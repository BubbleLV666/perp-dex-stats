Trading stats for any wallet on Entropy

Entropy is a trading front end of Hyperliquid with its own perp dex (io): pre-IPO and stock perpetuals such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Sandisk or Nvidia. Paste an address — the site queries the public Hyperliquid API directly and shows the margin account of that dex: balance, PnL, volume, fees, positions with liquidation prices, funding and a fill analysis.

Unofficial project, not affiliated with Entropy or Hyperliquid. Data comes from the public Hyperliquid API POST https://api.hyperliquid.xyz/info (dex io); it may lag or contain exchange errors.
